The polyfill ports phit’s Rust code to PHP. It does not reimplement the methods independently. Each port keeps the extension’s algorithm, constants, branch order, and floating-point operation order, so the two return the same bits.

Least squares

fit_ols scales the design columns to unit norm and factors the design by Householder QR, with the operation order of the extension’s linear-algebra library (nalgebra). The singular values of the small \(R\) factor, computed by a Jacobi sweep, equal the design’s. The extension’s rank rule, \(s \le \varepsilon \cdot \max(n, p) \cdot s_{\max}\), therefore makes the same rank decisions. Coefficients and covariances come from \(R\) by back-substitution. Weighted fits factor rows in decreasing-weight order, as the extension does.

Generalized linear models

fit_logistic and fit_poisson use damped Newton IRLS, and fit_probit uses Fisher scoring. Each iteration solves a weighted least-squares step with the same QR, halves the step until the likelihood does not decrease, and stops only after a small full step. The separation certificate, boundary handling, starting values for extreme offsets, and exception codes match the extension.

Mixed models

fit_mixed_intercept profiles the variance ratio as the extension does. fit_mixed and fit_lmer port the extension’s general linear mixed model, including its BOBYQA and Nelder–Mead optimizers. fit_logistic_mixed and fit_glmer port the likelihood (the Laplace approximation, or adaptive Gauss–Hermite quadrature with 2 to 61 points) and the BOBYQA search. The optimizers are ported from the same crate versions, so they take the same steps.

Distributions

The extension’s distribution layer is ported together with the parts of cdflib that it calls. These are TOMS 708 (incomplete beta), TOMS 654 (incomplete gamma), Cody’s normal CDF, and the dinvnr/dt1 quantile starts. Log-scale tails, Loader’s saddle-point densities, and the safeguarded quantile search are ported as well. The statrs routines that the estimators call (erfc, \(\ln \Gamma\), the regularized incomplete beta, and the inverse error function) are ported from the same crate version.

Rounding and fused multiply-add

PHP has no fused multiply-add. The extension’s matrix products on aarch64 accumulate with one, so the polyfill emulates a correctly rounded fused multiply-add (Boldo and Melquiond’s algorithm, from Dekker’s exact product and Knuth’s TwoSum). The emulation is exact unless a product or sum overflows or an error term is subnormal. Those operands use the unfused expression. Rust’s f64::round rounds half away from zero exactly, while PHP 8.3’s round() first rounds to 15 significant digits. The polyfill therefore rounds through its own exact function.

Validation

Inputs are validated in the extension’s order, so an input that is invalid in several ways raises the same exception code.
